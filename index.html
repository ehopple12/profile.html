About me

I've gone throughout life constantly feeling stagnant, complacent even, just because I was stuck in my head, unable to get out of misconceived notion that I wasn't going anywhere in my life. Then one day I met some folks living my dream of traveling the world, and they were only a couple years older than me. After that my life drastically changed. I began frequently going to the gym, gaining 45 pounds of muscle in a year, booking a flight to China, and just finding my identity in general. I've merely just begun coding, but I actually enjoy this more than I thought I would. Yes, it does drag, and it's hard at some points. Nonetheless, I give everything I do my very best effort, otherwise what's the point of doing it at all? I want to be the best I'm able to be, and I know I can be one of the best. I'm an individual who doesn't lack in the discipline department, I excel in self-criticism. I want to be the very best I can be everyday, so that's what I'm going to do.
